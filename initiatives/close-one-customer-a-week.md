## Why now

The ARR targets ($500K by 2026-12-31, $5M by 2027-02-28, $25M by
2027-12-31) only happen if new customers close every week. A week with no
close is the earliest sign the plan is slipping.

## Success criteria

- [ ] At least one new customer closed in every ISO week from 2026-W48 on.
- [ ] ARR at or above $500K on 2026-12-31.
- [ ] ARR at or above $5M on 2027-02-28.
- [ ] ARR at or above $25M on 2027-12-31.

## Approach

1. Log each close below as it happens: date, customer, ARR added, running ARR.
2. Weekly planning grades this every Sunday and posts the verdict in #sales.

## Dependencies

- Closed deals recorded here (and in Attio) so the weekly recap can count them.
