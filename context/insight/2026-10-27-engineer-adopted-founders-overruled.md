## Claim

For infrastructure like a context database, developer adoption is not the decision. Founders and CTOs can
and do overrule it.

## Evidence

- Internal: founder note, 2026-10-27. Nishkarsh: an engineer at a startup had signed up and was actively
  using HydraDB, then stopped. On a call he said: "my founders asked me to use something else".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-04. Nishkarsh: "We need to talk to CTO or closes persona."

Keep the account anonymous. Tension with [[insight/2026-09-10-pitch-the-team-not-the-exec]], where an exec
deferred to his team.

## Implication

Pair the open-source, developer-led motion with founder or CTO conversations early. Ask the engineer who
else has a say before they integrate.

## Use in messaging

Post: "Our best user stopped using us. Not because of the product. Because of one sentence: 'my founders
asked me to use something else.'"
