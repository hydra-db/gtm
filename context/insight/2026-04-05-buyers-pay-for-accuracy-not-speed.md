## Claim

For agent context, accuracy wins the deal and speed is the tie-breaker.

## Evidence

- A prospect building an IT service agent: "Latency matters but accuracy comes first."
  Internal: customer call recap, 2026-04-05
- An enterprise pitch plan put about 80% of the focus on data quality and labeling, with performance as
  a plus point; the team agreed performance impact should be emphasised less.
  Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-17
- A fintech's POC metrics were knowledge-error rate and tool calls, not latency.
  Internal: customer call recap, 2026-09-16

Validated: separate buyers (one's own words, another's choice of metrics) point the same way.

## Implication

Tension with the public speed-led positioning. In enterprise deals lead with accuracy and fewer tool calls;
keep speed for developer audiences, and then only as ranges with method.

## Use in messaging

Post: "We built the fastest graph engine we could. Customers asked about accuracy first."
