## Claim

"Context graph" is vendor language. Buyers describe the same need as company brain, institutional
knowledge, precedent, ontology, knowledge graph or agent memory.

## Evidence

- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-11. An ICP analysis Nishkarsh shared: "Most companies writing publicly
  about 'context graphs' are vendors and competitors ... Companies that build context graphs for their own
  product rarely use the term. They say 'company brain,' 'institutional knowledge,' 'precedent,'
  'ontology,' 'knowledge graph' or 'agent memory.'" Must-haves in the same doc: "Agents take actions or
  make decisions, not just Q&A over documents"; "Decisions depend on relationships between entities ...
  across systems"; "The product improves from past decisions (precedent, overrides, exceptions)".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-10-19. Nishkarsh dislikes the term himself, and on 2026-11-24 noted others
  "catching with different terms - context graph, sales graphs, etc etc".
- The ICP rewrite of 2026-11-13 adopted this finding ([[icp/hydradb]]).

## Implication

Write posts in buyer language ("company brain", "agent memory", "precedent") rather than "context graph".
Public "context graph" talk is a weak buying signal.

## Use in messaging

Post: "If you're building a context graph, you probably don't call it that."
