## Claim

SelfGraph, HydraDB's free open-source graph framework, is positioned as the on-ramp to HydraDB the way
Next.js is to Vercel. The launch led on LinkedIn, but the team flagged that it was barely posted about
afterwards.

## Evidence

- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-06. Positioning: "think what next.js did to vercel is what selfgraph is
  for hydradb. you can create free graphs for postgres or managing existing graphs in neo4j using this
  framework ... we just make the managing and development of graphs easy. once you're comfortable with
  using our free sdk you want to move over to hydradb? the framework's got you"
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-19. Restated: "what next.js is for building web apps; self graph is for
  building graphs, all tools you need to build graphs in one source sdk"
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-15. Goal: "Lets make sure every agent and dev on the planet that's ever
  thought of graphs (including Neo4j) goes through self graph. We get them first and then we get them with
  hydradb perf"
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-06. Launch plan: launch the open-source graph library on LinkedIn
  first; in the first hour, team members like and comment with substance ("not just 'congrats/cfbr'"),
  repost, and star the repo.
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-14. Channel advice: post from the founder's personal
  LinkedIn account and let the company page repost, for more eyeballs.
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-12. Gap: "we did selfgraph OSS, but posted nothing about it".
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-18. Follow-ups discussed: a Hacker News launch, a benchmark study
  "comparing all the ways to build a graph for your agent", and a tutorial video series.

## Implication

SelfGraph is fresh, under-told launch material: a product story that is free to try and not a sales
pitch, and it fits the 2026-11-24 "teach how to do X" direction.

## Use in messaging

- Post: "Next.js made Vercel easy to adopt. We built the same on-ramp for graphs." Point to the repo, not a
  demo call.
- Objection-style post for "we already use Postgres or a graph database": SelfGraph works on top of both.
- Do not describe any star growth as organic; do not announce follow-up dates that are not public yet.
