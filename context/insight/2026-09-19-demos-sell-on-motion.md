## Claim

Even for deeply technical infrastructure, the demo that converts is the visual one.

## Evidence

- Nishkarsh: "People want pretty colours and moving things For demos. That's what works" (shared with a
  LinkedIn post as the example). Internal: founder note, 2026-09-19
- The next day the dashboard gained a second, animated graph renderer alongside the existing graph view.
  Internal: engineering notes, 2026-09-21

## Implication

Show the graph being built and queried live; do not demo with JSON. Posts with a short clip of the graph
moving fit this.

## Use in messaging

Light founder post: "We built a database. Then we spent a week on how the graph moves on screen."
