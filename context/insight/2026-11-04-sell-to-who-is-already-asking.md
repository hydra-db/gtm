## Claim

Early infra GTM goes faster when you sell to teams already asking for graphs for agents, instead of trying
to "break instinct" in teams that don't see the problem yet.

## Evidence

- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-04. Nishkarsh: "We've been thinking about our GTM slightly
  inefficiently. We are thinking where to fit HydraDB instead of asking 'Who is asking for HydraDB?' ...
  while that might be great its an uphill battle ... We need to listen to people who already want us
  (graphs for agents) and just go to them with our findings." Two growth paths: open-source marketing,
  "which is working", and agent-native startups, targeted "not because its great revenue but because they
  are great problem testers." "We're running too many random experiments that will lead us nowhere ...
  we're doing only 3 things".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-06. Nishkarsh: be right with about five design accounts.
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-19: "Forget all random experiments and focus on fixing real stuff even if
  we have to be patient about finding the next use case."
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24: "are we going after companies that need a context graph or are we
  going for companies where we need to generate the demand for context graphs"
- Internal: founder note, 2026-07-24, an earlier version: "we're doing too many things this week but not
  producing asymmetric outputs"

## Implication

Narrow outreach to accounts already showing graph or memory intent; treat agent-native startups as design
partners.

## Use in messaging

Founder-lesson post, "something I got wrong in November": "We were asking where our product fits. Wrong
question." Do not name the target companies.
