## Claim

HydraDB's search numbers looked healthy but were brand searches: strip out "hydra" queries and organic
search drove 47 clicks in three months. Blog visitors reached the product dashboard at 0.25%, versus 5.72%
for homepage visitors. Programmatic comparison pages that share one intent cannibalise each other; one
strong page beats dozens of variants.

## Evidence

- Data audit (Internal: team discussion, 2026-09-11):
  - "Headline numbers look healthy, 6,190 clicks at 14.4% CTR over three months. Filter out queries
    containing 'hydra' and it is 47 clicks from 4,340 impressions at average position 27.6."
  - "Homepage visitors reach the product dashboard at 5.72%. Blog visitors reach it at 0.25%, 18 of 7,161
    over 90 days. The 25 highest-traffic posts produced three dashboard visits between them."
  - Three top pages held 21,772 search impressions at average positions 1.93 to 4.18, but earned only 88
    clicks between them (0.40% CTR, against the homepage's 27.4%).
- Blog growth for contrast: 12.8K impressions and 121 clicks for all published blogs (Internal: team
  discussion, 2026-09-04); later clicks up 123% and impressions up 75% month on month, CTR 1.06%
  (Internal: team discussion, 2026-10-13).
- Content audit: "many pages answer the same question for the same reader." Four graph databases each got
  a section on 26 to 32 of the 57 "best X for Y" pages, and 40 of those pages had their own "how to
  choose" section. "When the modifier doesn't change what the reader needs, one intent ends up split
  across many URLs, and none of them gets strong enough to rank or be cited." "Distributed graph database"
  gets about 100 US searches a month; "Best graph database for AI agents" has no measurable search demand
  of its own. Internal: team discussion, 2026-10-06
- Site audit: about 33 pages worth retaining, of which 12 get rewritten and 11 updated; use cases
  organised by what teams build rather than by industry. Internal: team discussion, 2026-10-02
- Founder reset: "We don't have to start with a frame of reference of 200 posts. We can start by
  internalizing that we need to do 20 posts well and the other 180 can wait." Internal: founder note,
  2026-10-01
- Growth note: if articles get no traction on accounts that otherwise average many likes, the issue is
  likely the articles themselves. Internal: team discussion, 2026-06-05

## Implication

Volume content (listicles, competitor-alternative pages) grows impressions, not pipeline. Fewer, deeper
pages, each with its own demand and intent, plus founder-led distribution, are the bet.

## Use in messaging

- Founder-lesson post: "Our SEO dashboard said 6,190 clicks. 47 of them were from people who had not
  already heard of us."
- "7,161 people read our blog in 90 days. 18 of them opened the product."
- Marketing lesson for founders: "We had 57 'best X for Y' pages. None of them ranked." Do not name the
  graph databases or any outside agency.
