## Claim

Enterprise and mid-market buyers do not buy "a graph platform". They buy one named graph for one
workflow, proven on their data.

## Evidence

Two independent accounts, reported the same day through different people:
- Internal: sales notes, 2026-11-24. An enterprise services firm tied its commitment to HydraDB building
  one specific graph (devices, for an IT service management workflow).
- Internal: sales notes, 2026-11-24. A second prospect tied its commitment to proving one use case: a sales
  graph built from CRM, call notes and email.
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24. Nishkarsh: "its like vectorDBs - how do you define a template for
  what goes in. our templates are data connectors imo"

Neither account is a customer or a public reference.

## Implication

Lead demos and pilots with a named graph (sales graph, device graph, contract graph) built from the buyer's
own connectors, under a fixed POC scope and clock.

## Use in messaging

Post hook: "Two prospects told us the same thing in one day: prove one graph on our data, not the
platform." Never call them customers, never name or describe either account, and say nothing about deal
status.
