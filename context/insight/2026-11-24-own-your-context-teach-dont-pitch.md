## Claim

For teams not yet sold on graphs, the story is not "fast graphs" but "own your context and memory", and
developer infrastructure grows by teaching a pattern, not by announcing a hosted product.

## Evidence

- Positioning, 2026-11-24: "for the second half the story isnt fast graphs or whatever - the story is 'own
  your context & memory'" / "build with primitives that you can use to assemble context for your agents"; "the idea is going to evolve from
  the tech i.e. graphs to what is that graphs do for you".
- Not a memory company, 2026-11-24: "we're not a memory company. we're the company that lets you build
  memory on top of us"; "if you talk to 100 devs all 90 will tell you 'we want to own their memory'" (his
  estimate).
- Teach the pattern, 2026-11-24: "for eg - langchain grew by telling people 'how to do rag' they didnt say
  'we are a hosted rag solution'" / "that's the market we need to tap into" / "agents have reached a point
  where they can self assemble most things (granted product has to work)" / "so our marketing should be 'how
  to do x', fully aligned" / "that's always been the hardest part for any dev tools company"
- SelfGraph as the free on-ramp: "think what next.js did to vercel is what selfgraph is for hydradb"
  (2026-11-06).
- Confusion this solves (2026-11-21): typing "hydradb" in a coding agent "autobiases the project towards the
  open source hydradb instead of hydradb hosted and both products have different usage shapes"
- Category label, same day: "turbopuffer for graphDBs" is the latest framing for graph-native buyers
  ([[global/positioning]]); "own your context" is the line for everyone else.

## Implication

Posts should teach a build pattern ("how to build X with graphs": memory, sales graph, company brain),
written so a coding agent could follow them, and let HydraDB appear as the tool used rather than lead with
product claims.

## Use in messaging

- Post: "The best dev-tool companies didn't sell a product. They taught a pattern."
- Post: "We're not a memory company. We're the company you build your memory on."
- "How to build X" posts, and the repositioning-away-from-memory-vendor post for the week of 2026-11-24.
