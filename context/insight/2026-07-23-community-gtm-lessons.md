## Claim

Developer communities reward expertise, not volume; small, technical formats beat expensive sponsorships.

## Evidence

- Reddit: an outsourced posting service gave low-quality conversion, so the program moved in-house.
  Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-07. Rule: about 80% helpful comments and 20% HydraDB mentions, to
  avoid looking like spam. Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-23. AI-written replies were not good enough
  for nuanced communication. Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-12. Around 110 comments in one push got
  limited engagement. Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-14
- Events: a quoted sponsorship cost several times what similar 200-attendee events cost to run
  directly. Internal: team discussion, 2026-06-30. A co-hosted hackathon had about 30% of attendees leave
  early. Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-14. Paper-reading nights beat a larger established event on
  signups, but 30-slide decks were too long. Internal: team discussion, 2026-08-05
- Website: visitors explored docs but rarely signed up. Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-17

## Implication

Invest in paper readings, a Discord community and champion spotlights; skip pricey sponsorships.

## Use in messaging

Founder GTM lessons post with the 80/20 rule and paper-reading lessons. Do not name any outside service,
co-host or other company's event, and do not quote event budgets.
