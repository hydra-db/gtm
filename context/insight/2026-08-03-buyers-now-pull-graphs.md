## Claim

Graph engineering crossed from education to demand in 2026: some buyers now arrive wanting graphs before
they know the use case.

## Evidence

- Nishkarsh: "just wrapped up a convo with a big potential customer that came to us not because of the
  fact that they wanted to use hydradb but because of the general sentiment of graph engineering on social
  media. and instead of us selling to them they wanted to work with us to 'figure' out graph use cases
  with them. weird feeling in terms of going from helping people realize the importance of graphs to
  people forcing their teams to adopt graphs." Internal: founder note, 2026-08-03
- A prospect on a call (independent): "vectorDB isn't enough and we need knowledge graphs for our agents
  to work". Internal: customer call recap, 2026-10-15
- A company building an enterprise search product on HydraDB. Internal: founder note, 2026-10-07

Validated: the founder's account and a separate prospect's own statement on a different call point the
same way.

## Implication

Sell co-discovery: help the buyer find the first one or two graph use cases, then scope a fixed POC
([[insight/2026-08-05-pocs-need-a-fixed-scope-and-clock]]).

## Use in messaging

Founder "this changed" post. Strong first-person hook in Nishkarsh's own words. Do not name the prospect
and do not call it a customer.
