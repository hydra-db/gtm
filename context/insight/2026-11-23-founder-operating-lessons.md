## Claim

A handful of operating rules Nishkarsh stated in November 2026, usable as founder-lesson posts. Voice:
blunt, binary, speed over polish.

## Evidence

- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-22. Messy notes win: experiment learnings "should be just running notes
  from every experiment" and "should be messy - if you're spending time (like one of those front benchers)
  colour coding and beautifying everything in your notebook we're ngmi".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-19. Binary quality bar: "do we do this REALLY well - yes or no? if yes -
  let's do this right now. if not, how do we do this". And: you can say "we do this", but if the user has
  to wait 12 hours for results, you don't.
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-23. Connectors are the hard part: "despite all our efforts of doing
  weekly testing, dedicated attention to connectors, spending months on connectors we're still running
  into these issue. Not acceptable ... figure out a way to pre-emptively monitor and fix connector issues".
  The lesson the team drew: monitoring has to cover production, not only staging.
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24. Don't be the blocker: "generally dont ask me for these things because
  i end up becoming a blocker and we can come off as slow for customer support".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-16. Founder onboards signups: "Hi! Saw you signed up for HydraDB. What
  are you looking to achieve? If you're planning on integrating let me know."

## Implication

Voice material. In posts, keep the connector story as "a connector broke" with no customer, timing or
impact details. No revenue targets in posts.

## Use in messaging

- Post: "If your experiment notes are color-coded, you're running too few experiments."
- Post: "We spent months on connectors. One broke anyway. Monitoring that only runs on staging is not
  monitoring."
- Post: "I still message every new signup myself."
