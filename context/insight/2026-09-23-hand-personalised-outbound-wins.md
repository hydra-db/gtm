## Claim

For technical buyers of AI infrastructure, automated sequences barely register; personal, specific
messages and LinkedIn do the work.

## Evidence

- GTM review (an assistant's summary of an internal doc): about 0.1% genuine replies across 5,350
  sequenced leads, and every real reply came from hand-personalised messages. A LinkedIn campaign was
  getting 23% accepts and 14% replies. Internal: team discussion, 2026-09-23
- Sales day log: one sequencing tool's replies were negative; test copy on another got one positive
  reply. "This is why we need an A/B test though, and not rely entirely on 1 copy." The same day, an
  agent that found event attendees and sent intro messages booked two meetings. Internal: sales notes,
  2026-08-27

## Implication

Founder-led, personal, signal-based outreach plus LinkedIn content beats volume sequencing at this stage.
