## Claim

"Memory for agents" became a named category in 2026 with five or more look-alike products compared in every
roundup. Competition happens in open source and third-party roundups, not paid ads. A product pitched as
"memory" gets lost; one pitched as what you build memory on has room.

## Evidence

- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-21. Competitor tracker reading: none of the three tracked competitors had active Meta ads.
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-23. Tracker reading: other memory products appear in nearly every 2026 "alternatives" and "X vs Y" roundup, and some raised fresh seed
  or Series A rounds in early 2026 (public announcements).
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-19. A team member, on a public post skeptical of graphs: "I want to
  see every investor say 'we don't invest in that area, Hydra is already the default'".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24. Nishkarsh rejected a draft framing HydraDB as an alternative to a
  memory product.

All numbers are a bot's point-in-time reading of third-party sources. Re-verify before publishing any.
Named products and figures live in [[alternative/agent-memory-layers]].

## Implication

Don't enter the memory roundup on its terms. Position on the layer beneath (graph, multi-source context,
owned memory).

## Use in messaging

Post angle: "Agent memory is a crowded shelf. None of those products is the problem." Never
name any product.
