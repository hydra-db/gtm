## Claim

For HydraDB, open source is the main top of funnel: free frameworks pull developers in, and the licence was
loosened to reduce adoption friction.

## Evidence

- Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-24. Original plan: AGPL plus a commercial enterprise licence, a dual
  model.
- Internal: public post, 2026-08-12. Core repo open-sourced: "this is the first time ive seen a company
  able to build a database with such speed and quality ... we built infra that scales ... and then open
  sourced it". (On 2026-06-30 the product was still not open source.)
- Internal: public post, 2026-09-17. "Glean, but open sourced" announced.
- Internal: founder note, 2026-08-12. Funnel rule: teams that say no to direct outreach get pushed to the
  open-source bucket "so that they can at least try us out for free".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-04: "OSS marketing - which is working."
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-15. Licence switch: "we switched licenses to make it [easy for] people to
  adopt it. elv2 is better than agpl"
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-15. SelfGraph goal: "Lets make sure every agent and dev on the planet
  that's ever thought of graphs ... goes through self graph. We get them first and then we get them with
  hydradb perf"
- Internal: engineering notes, 2026-11-15. A Postgres extension went live in the open-source repo.
- Internal: engineering notes, 2026-11-21. Side effect: coding agents default to the open-source project
  when asked for "hydradb", and the open-source and hosted products "have different usage shapes".

## Implication

Behind-the-scenes posts on licence choice and OSS-led growth are on-voice and concrete.

## Use in messaging

- "Why we changed our open source licence" post (AGPL plan in July 2026, ELv2 from 2026-11-15).
- "A no from sales should become a yes to the free repo."
