## Claim

Giving the hosted product away to seed-stage companies did not create revenue; the free tier belongs in
open source, and the hosted product is for companies that pay.

## Evidence

- Early 2026 diagnosis: various seed companies were interested but unwilling to pay, and free pricing for
  seed companies was undermining the business model. "Free is not a company strategy": the company needs
  customers who value quality. Internal: founder note, 2026-01-22
- Mid-2026 decision (independent, later): open source is free for pre-Series A startups; paid contracts
  start at Series B and beyond. Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-24
- Teams that decline outreach are pointed to the open-source repo so they can at least try HydraDB for
  free. Internal: sales notes, 2026-08-12
- "HydraDB is fully open source." The hosted free plan was deprecated in early October 2026.
  Internal: founder note, 2026-09-29

Validated: the diagnosis (January) and the decision trail (July to September) are separate sources
pointing the same way.

## Implication

Segment by stage: early teams self-host the open-source core (licensed ELv2 since 2026-11-15); paying
companies buy hosted or enterprise.

## Use in messaging

Founder-lesson post: "We gave our product away to seed startups. They used it and almost nobody paid.
Here is what we changed." Do not quote revenue figures.
