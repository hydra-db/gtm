## Claim

Agent memory is becoming a feature; the company brain (an owned ontology across all company data) is the
larger buying reason.

## Evidence

- Team decision: focus shifting to company brain and ontology solutions; agent memory deprioritised but
  still supported based on market signals; enterprises seek private, fine-tuned LLMs, moving away from
  black-box AI. Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-25
- Nishkarsh: "we're the best stack to build company brain". Internal: founder note, 2026-07-23
- Later evolution: "we're not a memory company. we're the company that lets you build memory on top of
  us" and "the story is 'own your context & memory'" (see
  [[insight/2026-11-24-own-your-context-teach-dont-pitch]]). Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24

## Implication

Content targets "agent memory, ontology, company brain" for search and AI citation but leads with company
brain and owned context for business buyers. Note the drift: the website still lists agent memory among
use cases.

Founder strategy post on why a memory layer is not enough.
