## Claim

AI-generated content buys discoverability but costs credibility with technical buyers; founder-voice,
handcrafted content is worth the slower pace.

## Evidence

- Internal: founder note, 2026-10-23. Nishkarsh: "our blogs do come across as genAI garbage (but they were
  the only thing helping with discoverability)", and the plan going forward is a no-AI-slop approach with
  handcrafted blogs "that don't come across as silly or sloppy".
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-15. Nishkarsh: "the middle ground is i will de slopify it, you write it
  meticulously".
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-15. A website audit queued wording fixes across 11 blog posts.
- Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24. Nishkarsh on a drafted reply: "the response reads like ai lol".
- Internal: team discussion, 2026-08-03. Nishkarsh shared the public news that LinkedIn introduced a
  "seems like AI slop" button.

## Implication

LinkedIn drafts in Nishkarsh's voice must be specific and plain. Admitting the slop is itself a credible,
contrarian post.

## Use in messaging

Post: "Our blog was AI slop. It was also our only source of traffic. Here's what we're doing instead."
