## Claim

An open-ended POC is a slow no. Fix the scope, success metrics and decision date before starting.

## Evidence

- Sales review: POCs had no fixed timeline, which caused scope creep and at least one lost deal. Decision:
  one owner for all POCs, with fixed timelines. Internal: team discussion, 2026-08-05
- Nishkarsh: new features get built only after the POC, to avoid scope bloat. Internal: founder note,
  2026-10-07
- Applied: a later POC was scoped up front with target error-rate and tool-call metrics, a few weeks of
  runtime and a written decision date. Internal: sales notes, 2026-09-16

Validated: the sales-team review of a lost deal and the founder's separate rule point the same way.

## Implication

Every POC starts with written success metrics, a timeline and a decision date, scoped to one named graph
([[insight/2026-11-24-commit-after-one-proven-graph]]).

## Use in messaging

Founder lesson post: "We lost a deal to a POC with no end date." No amounts, no account names, no POC
terms.
