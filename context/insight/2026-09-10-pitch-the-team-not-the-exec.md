## Claim

At AI-native target accounts, infrastructure evaluation sits with engineers. Pitching the executive cold
fails before the product is named; a strong referral into the team is the entry.

## Evidence

- Nishkarsh: "i tried pitching to him and he said 'my team takes decisions for me' before i could tell
  him what hydradb was (which is fair they get pitched a lot). i just need a very solid referral telling
  them why they should use us and i need 5 mins uninterrupted with them." Internal: founder note,
  2026-09-10

Tension: later an engineer who had adopted HydraDB was overruled by his founders
([[insight/2026-10-27-engineer-adopted-founders-overruled]]). Engineers run the evaluation; leaders can
still veto it.

## Implication

Target engineers who own the agent harness, use referrals for exec access, and bring the founder or CTO in
before integration.

## Use in messaging

Founder-lesson post: "I got cut off before I could say what we do. He was right." Do not name the person
or company.
