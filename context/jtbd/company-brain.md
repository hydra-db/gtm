## Job

When _knowledge walks out the door or sits in silos_, I want _a company brain built on my own ontology, in my own environment, by models I control_, so that _every team and agent answers from the same definitions_.

## Current solution

Enterprise search products; data-platform and ontology stacks; an existing knowledge graph and ontology; internal wikis.

## Gap

- A bank: "mitigating knowledge loss from RM attrition" (2026-09-30)
- A fintech: teams reading the same warehouse query differently (2026-08-27)
- A company building an enterprise-search alternative on top of HydraDB (2026-10-07)
- An AI-native CRM's lower-risk first use case: "internal company knowledge brain" (2026-09-22)
- A talent platform porting its existing knowledge graph and ontology (2026-07-17)

## Evidence

HydraDB reprioritised GTM toward "company brain and ontologies" in July 2026. The enterprise message: "Own your model. Own your ontology. Own your data." Benchmark: 88.7 on a company-brain set of 511,958 documents, many "duplicated, outdated or contradictory" ([[proof/retrieval-accuracy-benchmarks]]). Use-case name in sales: "company brain (knowledge graph)".
