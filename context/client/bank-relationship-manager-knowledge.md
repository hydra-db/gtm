## Profile

A bank with a recently hired AI lead. Not a fit at the time because of cloud-provider requirements. Not a public reference.

## Use case

Use cases they raised: "RM productivity: lead prioritization & customer context management"; "Knowledge management: mitigating knowledge loss from RM attrition"; voice AI; internal company brain.

## Outcome

Disqualified on cloud fit (2026-09-30).

## Story

They compared HydraDB with general-purpose graph databases and data-platform ontology tooling, and asked HydraDB to clarify its edge over their existing data stack. Infrastructure fit ended it before the use case was tested. Lesson: qualify on deployment constraints in the first call. A new AI leader is a good signal, but the stack they inherit decides.
