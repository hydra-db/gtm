## Profile

A large B2B data company. Not cleared to name, and not a customer. Describe as "a B2B data company".

## Use case

Identity graphs: "If we uncover relations across millions of visitors then we think that data is much more enriched and valuable for [them] & its agents."

## Outcome

Target, not result: "If 6-8 visitors out of every 10 visitors are de-anonymized real-time or the day they sign up you already knew what they were doing we can unlock massive insights!" Approach: "Let's pick one prove success and validate the idea with one and move forward". Early results were shared internally (numbers not recorded).

## Story

Nishkarsh: moving "impressively fast for their size". Lesson: prove one use case before opening a second.

## Quotes

- "We see gains with identity graphs, I suggest we prove that out before moving onto a different use case." (Nishkarsh)
