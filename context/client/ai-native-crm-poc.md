## Profile

A startup building an AI-native CRM, pre-launch in late 2026, technically strong. Not a public reference.

## Use case

Two, in order of risk: (1) "internal company knowledge brain (lower risk)", (2) "CRM memory layer (higher impact)".

## Outcome

POC planning stage. No result recorded.

## Story

They aggregate deal data across chat, email and internal notes and struggle with:
- "Temporal deal tracking (e.g., deal value changes from $1K to $40K)"
- "Entity relationship queries across scattered data sources"
- "Agent iteration inefficiency due to incomplete context"
- "Scalability concerns as customer data grows post-launch"

The pattern: start with the internal brain to build trust, then put HydraDB in the product.
