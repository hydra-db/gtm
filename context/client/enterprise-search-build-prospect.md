## Story

Nishkarsh asked engineering to sort every promise he made on the call into two buckets: what can be achieved today ("with jugaad engineering or in general") and what needs net new features, because "we will create new features only after POC (to avoid scope bloat) and once they sign enterprise contract." Lesson: separate what works today from what you are promising, before the POC starts.
