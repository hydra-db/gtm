## Profile

A large talent marketplace. Not a public reference.

## Use case

Port their existing knowledge graph and ontology onto HydraDB; use HydraDB to parse incoming records; data connectors including their data warehouse.

## Outcome

In progress mid 2026. No result recorded.

## Story

Nishkarsh's pitch plan (2026-07-17): show the applied AI work in numbers plus a short roadmap, show connectors, show they can port their knowledge graph, "and then we will slightly segue into performance numbers as to why running their ontology on top of us is the best thing". "80% of the focus will be on the quality data labeling, all of those things, and that 20% is a plus point to be super performant." Team notes agreed: performance impact is minimal, so emphasise it less. Lesson: for ontology buyers, quality sells and speed is the bonus.

## Quotes

- Nishkarsh to the team: "P.s. I don't usually say this, but with [this account] we can move a "little" slower BUT get everything right in the first shot. Quality of our work matters a lot this time."
