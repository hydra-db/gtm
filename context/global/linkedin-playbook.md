## Summary

Three posts a week from Nishkarsh's personal account (Mon, Wed, Fri), posted on weekday PST mornings, short, specific, no AI slop, no personal-name tags. Treat X as the leading indicator: what goes viral there gets adapted for LinkedIn. Posts exist to drive sign ups. Teach "how to do x"; do not pitch.

## Detail

### Cadence and timing

- "i want to do 3 posts from personal account and 2-3 from company account. whatever we post on linkedin we can translate to twitter as well" (Internal: founder note, 2026-10-01).
- "schedule all these posts to go out from my linkedin M, W, F starting NEXT week" (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-19).
- One post per day maximum: "DO NOT post all posts at once. Only post once a day." (Internal: founder note, 2026-10-13).
- Time zone: "Are we planning on posting IST am? Let's avoid that and only post PST AM." (Internal: founder note, 2026-07-27). Team observation: "better engagement on weekdays around 10-10:30am PT" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-08-01). Weekend posts get buried by Monday posts (Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-17). Launch lesson: "making sure to launch 7am" (Internal: founder note, 2026-04-28).
- Missing days get noticed: "no post on LinkedIn today?" (Internal: founder note, 2026-08-05).

### Length and style

- After a weaker post: "Let's try to reduce the word length" and "can we try different post timings" (Internal: founder note, 2026-07-22).
- No AI slop. He shared the "LinkedIn introduces a 'seems like AI slop' button" story with the content team twice (Internal: founder note, 2026-08-03).
- Paper-reading and session posts: "please don't write generic stuff. break down the paper, discussions, and 3 main learnings from that discussions + any FAQs answered" (Internal: founder note, 2026-10-15).
- "less is more" for video and content (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-22).
- No hype adjectives ("cracked") (Internal: founder note, 2026-07-28).
- No speed or cost multiples, no ARR or revenue, no unpermitted customer names: see [[proof/performance-and-cost-claims]] and the client files.

### Tagging

- "Company tagging is okay but no personal name tagging pls. At all." (Internal: founder note, 2026-08-12). This later rule overrides an earlier July request to tag people in interview posts. A team observation on 2026-07-10 was that tagging companies raises reach.

### Source of topics

- "LinkedIn audience is a lagging indicator of what's happening on Twitter" and "keep posting things going viral on Twitter to LinkedIn" (Internal: founder note, 2026-08-01).
- Follow accounts that go viral on X and adapt what works for LinkedIn (Internal: founder note, 2026-08-15).
- Format he pointed to as the target: a post about Google's algorithm shrinking 31GB, "or what mem0 does on their twitter. regular content / blogs being pushed out" (Internal: founder note, 2026-05-27).
- His planned series (2026-11-19): "im going to post about real world events and help people predict outcomes using 'conspiracy boards' modeling all world events i find interesting and talking about the related entities in that" (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-19).
- Teach, don't pitch: "our marketing should be 'how to do x'" (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24).
- Video format: "smaller video on my linkedin that can be linked back to the main youtube video via comments" (Internal: founder note, 2026-07-28).
- Cross-posting X articles: "pls let me know when twitter articles go out. will post the same from my linkedin account" (Internal: founder note, 2026-06-03).

### Calls to action

- Launch CTA: "the cta has to be use us or something" and "we wont be usign this launch to talk about the conference at all" (Internal: founder note, 2026-03-09).
- CTA: point to dashboard.hydradb.com or the open-source repo.
- Goal metric for all content is sign ups: "Your goal is optimize for sign ups" (Internal: founder note, 2026-07-24).

### Watch-outs

- Engagement must be real; Nishkarsh flagged a post whose reactions-to-impressions ratio looked off (Internal: founder note, 2026-08-25). Never buy engagement.
- Do not describe launch reach as purely organic.

### Topics we do not post about

Set by Nishkarsh on 2026-11-24. Do not draft posts on, or cite numbers for: GitHub star counts (ours or anyone's), pricing and plans, YC Demo Day, or data retention.

### Evidence from our own posts

Condensed from [[insight/2026-11-23-what-works-on-linkedin-and-x]] (confidence: hypothesis). Numbers are team-reported.

- **Best LinkedIn result:** the launch post "We've raised $6.5M to kill vector databases" (2026-03-12): "Nearing 1,400 likes & 80k impressions on the post now" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-03-13; public post: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nishkarsh-srivastava_weve-raised-65m-to-kill-vector-databases-activity-7437867439628963840-crb-/). The team's read: "the rage bait in the copy/script definitely seems to be working from a virality approach" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-03-12).
- **Same launch on X:** "went viral with 3.7M views on our first launch itself" (Internal: sales notes, 2026-05-08). Hypothesis: team-reported, no screenshot on record. Do not add the LinkedIn and X numbers together.
- **Why the launch spread (Nishkarsh, 2026-04-28):** "someone like jeff dean reposting somehow made the entire team at google like and repost our launch video. that is what made it go viral" (a public repost); formula "a hook in the video / enticing title / and making sure to launch 7am"; "Post the 1M impressions mark is when you start seeing actual traction and usage for your product" (Internal: founder note, 2026-04-28).
- **Inbound from one post:** "2 people from my network reached out after that one post asking for hydradb trials" (Internal: founder note, 2026-07-11).
- **Team posts beat blogs:** a team member's post got "1000+ impressions with 30 something reactions in 7 hours ... a blog would take a month to get here organically" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-04). A hackathon announcement post: "5K impressions and counting" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-14). A devrel teammate went viral more than once riffing on research news (Internal: team discussion, 2026-04-16).
- **Baseline:** average LinkedIn impressions "~ 2500" per post in early September (Internal: team discussion, 2026-09-06).
- **Fell flat:** the longer definitional post "Your business does not have one agreed definition..." "Got slightly less engament" (2026-07-22); externally written articles were "okayish, underwhelming from a comments PoV" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-06-04); an open-source launch got no post at all: "we did selfgraph OSS, but posted nothing about it" (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-12).
- **Video:** "much of the impressions traffic last 2 weeks have compounded for the podcast/video posts we made" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-08-23), a reading that was disputed the same day.
- **Other Nishkarsh post hooks on record (no metrics):** "Agents are dumb token predictors..." (2026-04-27); "Perplexity just shipped a memory system that..." (2026-07-10); "3 months ago we were 4 people, today we're..." (2026-07-18); first podcast post (2026-07-20); "LangChain had 65 million downloads a month..." (2026-08-12); "Graph now means a lot of things in AI..." (2026-08-19). (Internal: public post, dates inline)
- **Implication:** default weekly posts will not go viral on their own. Reach comes from a contrarian hook (similarity is not relevance), a news moment already trending on X, short video, weekday PT-morning timing, and real comments in the first hour. Big numbers come from concentrated launch moments, one per month, not a drip of mini-launches (Internal: team discussion, 2026-04-13).
