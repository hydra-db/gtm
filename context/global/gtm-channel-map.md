## Summary

Founder network first, then developer channels (LinkedIn, X, GitHub, Reddit, HN, Product Hunt, AI-assistant visibility), backed by signal-driven outbound. "ONLY the companies that need attention will hit prospecting."

## Detail

**Channel tiers (Nishkarsh, 2026-07-14)**
- Tier 0 (Nishkarsh): "word of mouth + personal outreach to networks"
- Tier 1: LinkedIn, X, Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, YouTube / online course, "Google, Claude / ChatGPT", Lobste.rs, dev.to / Hashnode, "Community - dinners & discord"
- Tier 2: YouTube shorts, TikTok, Instagram

**Developer social plan (2026-07-09)**: YouTube creators, Reddit and dev.to, LinkedIn on Nishkarsh's account, technical blogs "for trust", Hacker News, Discord, Product Hunt, "word of mouth - via github", then sales / outbound.

**Peer playbooks studied (2026-07-10)**: an agent-tooling company posts "genuine, well-written technical comparisons in subreddits like r/ClaudeAI, r/LangChain, r/mcp, r/LocalLLaMA, with the product coming up naturally instead of being pitched"; a database company runs a short-form video tutorial series natively on LinkedIn hosted by its evangelist. "Net-net there's no direct playbook to copy in our space."

**Outbound segments (2026-08-12)**: sequences split by segment (hackathon participants, SF founders, AI leaders outside SF, competitor users, organisations building AI, enterprise orgs), with a cap on contacts in flight per sequence. A LinkedIn campaign to AI leaders saw "23% accepts and 14% replies" (Internal: sales notes, 2026-09-23).

**Funnel definition (2026-08-12)**: "Impressions > Click > Sign Up > MQL", with named-account targeting for any signup on a company domain. Rule (2026-10-26): "we should not [do] outbound for all sign ups".

**November 2026 plan (Nishkarsh, 2026-11-19)**: Hacker News launch, YC Bookface launch, own launch, Product Hunt, newsletters, a video engine posting 2 to 3 times a week to X and YouTube shorts, LinkedIn content from Nishkarsh. Outbound: GitHub and other signals "refreshed every 24 hours", LinkedIn and email sequencing, "ONLY the companies that need attention will hit prospecting".

**Social reply rule (Nishkarsh, 2026-11-14)**: on X leads, "dont overthink this so much / just reply to people on x / and be nice".

**Contextual outreach (Nishkarsh, 2026-11-05)**: "We literally reach out to the AI natives with contextual reach out about oh I know that you use PineCone and I am also YC please advise".
