## Summary

Started as an AI workplace search product (founded March 2023), became Cortex (agent memory), launched as HydraDB with a $6.5M raise on 2026-03-12, open-sourced its core graph database on 2026-08-12, joined YC F26, switched the open-source license from the July AGPL plan to ELv2 in November 2026, and moved into a new SF office on 2026-11-25.

## Detail

### Funding

- Raise: **$6.5M**, announced 2026-03-12 in the public launch post "We've raised $6.5M to kill vector databases" (Internal: public post, 2026-03-12). Use $6.5M; it is the public figure.
- Do not name individual investors in posts. YC is the only backer to name.

### Timeline, oldest first

- **March 2023, first company.** AI workplace search: "your AI assistant that answers all work-related questions. Think of it as ChatGPT synced, in real-time, with your apps and knowledge base." Origin line: "all three co-founders have been fortunate enough to work in fast-paced work environment but often things were messy, strutures were being built and broken repetitely leading to a mess with documentation and knowledge utilization." (Internal: founder note, 2024-05-10)
- **2025-03-03, a demo moment.** "i was giving a demo and the person asked "can my team summarise its learnings" -- i asked this vague question. i was insanely fascinated & impressed and the person watching the demo had his mind blown away." (Internal: founder note, 2025-03-03)
- **2025-09-09.** "its demo day for a bunch of our customers doing y combinator today" (Internal: founder note, 2025-09-09)
- **2026-01-22, outside diagnosis.** An advisor's read on Cortex: "absent from current agent memory conversations", "Benchmark publication essential for credibility", "Missing testimonials and case studies" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-01-22)
- **2026-02-27, Cortex launch brief.** "Framing the gap between RAG and true long-term memory for LLM agents ... We want to own the word association to the word 'context'" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-02-27)
- **2026-03-12, HydraDB launch and $6.5M announcement.** Launch post above.
- **2026-04-05.** An ITSM prospect reached out because "HydraDB's recent funding announcement prompted this conversation." (Internal: customer call recap, 2026-04-05)
- **2026-06-25 to 2026-06-30.** SEO blogs "gained 200 Google clicks"; bug bounty had "over 50 participants on day one", then "100+ signups" (Internal: team discussion, 2026-06-25 to 2026-06-30).
- **2026-07-07.** A context-management platform moved its graph from a general-purpose graph database to HydraDB to onboard its own first paying customer.
- **2026-07-24/25, all-hands.** Several recent go-lives reported (none is a public reference). Open-source release planned for August (Internal: team discussion, 2026-07-24).
- **2026-08-12, open-source release.** "github.com/hydra-db/hydradb" with "this is the first time ive seen a company able to build a database with such speed and quality." (Internal: founder note, 2026-08-12). "Hack Hydra" online hackathon 2026-08-12 to 2026-08-20. Star counts in [[proof/open-source-momentum]].
- **2026-09-10.** Token volume reached a billion tokens per day, about 2.5 months after launch (see [[proof/token-volume]]).
- **2026-09-14/15, YC.** HydraDB joined YC F26. Founder on timing: "we got into YC way before our fundraise but decided to do it when we had a finished product" (Internal: founder note, 2026-10-20).
- **2026-09-17.** "announcing Glean, but open sourced: github.com/hydra-db/open-glean" (Internal: founder note, 2026-09-17). Codex and Cursor plugins powered by HydraDB released as open source.
- **2026-09-27 to 2026-10-04.** Repo crossed 10,000 stars and was "#1 trending" on GitHub (see [[proof/open-source-momentum]]). September changelog (posted 2026-10-04) shipped permission-aware search, eight new connectors, `llm_prompt` on every query, custom extraction instructions, a redesigned dashboard and on-prem SLMs for KG extraction in Enterprise (Internal: public post, 2026-10-04).
- **2026-10-02.** Hosted free plan deprecated: "HydraDB is fully open source. Free plan on our platform will be deprecated on October 2, 2026." (Internal: public post, 2026-09-29)
- **2026-11-01.** Public benchmark scorecard prepared for the website (see [[proof/retrieval-accuracy-benchmarks]]).
- **2026-11-03 to 2026-11-15, license change.** License changed to Elastic License v2 (2026-11-03). Nishkarsh, 2026-11-15: "we switched licenses to make it people to adopt it easily. elv2 is better than agpl." As of 2026-11-15 the license is ELv2; it supersedes the July 2026 AGPL plan (Internal: engineering notes, 2026-11-03; founder note, 2026-11-15).
- **2026-11-05.** Weekly all-hands started "to share company wide wins / losses ... across: coreDB, product, applied AI, and customer front". Team launch on LinkedIn and X (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-05).
- **2026-11-09.** The team tracked big-company knowledge-graph moves posted publicly on X (Netflix, GitHub, Grafana, NVIDIA, Atlassian) (Internal: team discussion, 2026-11-09).
- **2026-11-15.** "pg_hydradb - is live in oss repo!" (Internal: engineering notes, 2026-11-15). Learning-first meetings with several large AI and software companies ("more learning less pitch"); AWS re:Invent prep. These are conversations, not customers.
- **2026-11-19.** Self-hosted small language models live on prod (see [[proof/self-hosted-slms-in-production]]).
- **2026-11-20.** New query engine made the default in the open-source core; legacy engine retired (see [[proof/open-source-momentum]]).
- **2026-11-25.** Team starts working from a new office in San Francisco (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-24). Team spread across SF and India: "We're growing as a team and we're across the world." (Internal: founder note, 2026-11-16)
