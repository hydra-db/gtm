## Source

Website visitor identification and intent scoring (Internal: team discussion, 2026-09-28); product signup alerts and usage (Internal: sales notes, 2026-11-10).

## Definition

Either:
1. A visitor whose role is senior engineering or platform (for example "Sr. Software Engineering Manager", "Senior Director of Platform Engineering") from a company that announced a round in recent weeks; the team called this "real money with the right persona".
2. A signup followed by rising activity from the same company: "he had signed up, and then I saw [the company's] team activity going up, hence I reached out."

## Weight

high for (1) with a named senior engineer; medium for (2)
